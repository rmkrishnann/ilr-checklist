Cover Letter Best Practice ▼
⚠ Your cover letter for each applicant should briefly explain and disclose:
  • Any notices from the Home Office (retraction notices, curtailment notices, refusal letters — even if issued in error)
  • Any correspondence from HMRC (tax investigations, penalties, status queries)
  • Any notices from DWP, local councils, NHS, or other government bodies that touch on immigration status, residency, or right to work
  • Any passport stamp errors or discrepancies (e.g. wrong dates from third-country transit)
  • Any previous visa refusals (UK or worldwide) and their resolution

Always include the original notice and the resolution/cancellation confirmation. Proactive disclosure is always better than the Home Office discovering undisclosed issues.
