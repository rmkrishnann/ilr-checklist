ILR Document Details & Summaries

What each document contains and why it is needed. No personal information included.

⚠ Disclaimer: This is guidance from personal experience — always refer to Home Office documents for any queries. If you have received any notices from the Home Office, HMRC, DWP, local council, or any UK government agency — even if issued in error — disclose them in your cover letter and include the original notice plus any resolution/cancellation confirmation.
Main Applicant Documents MAIN ▼
1. Current Passport (Bio Page)
Category: IdentityRequired: Mandatory
Scan of the biographical data page of the current valid passport. Proves identity, nationality, date of birth, and passport number. Must be valid at date of application.
2. Current Passport — Stamped Pages
Category: Identity / TravelRequired: Mandatory
Scans of all pages containing entry/exit stamps from the current passport. Helps the caseworker verify travel history and absences from the UK during the qualifying period.
3. Previous Passport — Stamped Pages
Category: Identity / TravelRequired: Mandatory
Scans of stamped pages from the previous (expired) passport. Important for showing entry/exit history during the earlier part of the qualifying period when the old passport was in use.
4. Biometric Residence Permits (All)
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Scans of all BRPs issued during the qualifying period. Shows continuous lawful permission to remain in the UK. Include both front and back if possible.
5. eVisa / Residence Permit Screenshot
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Screenshot from the UKVI online account showing current digital immigration status. Confirms live immigration permission and conditions.
6. eVisa Share Code
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Generated share code from the gov.uk "View and prove" service. Allows the caseworker to independently verify immigration status online.
7. Skilled Worker Application Submission Proof
Category: SW 21.2(i) EvidenceRequired: If applicable
Confirmation or receipt showing the date the original Skilled Worker visa application was submitted. Critical for proving the qualifying period start date falls within the COVID concession window (24 Jan 2020 – 30 Jun 2021).
8. Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)
Category: SW 21.2(i) EvidenceRequired: If applicable
Shows a valid CoS was assigned and supported the Skilled Worker application. One of the four conditions of SW 21.2(i) that must be met.
9. Biometrics Appointment Proof
Category: SW 21.2(i) EvidenceRequired: If applicable
Appointment confirmation showing biometrics were enrolled at a UKVCAS centre in support of the original Skilled Worker application. Supports in-country application claim.
10. UKVCAS Submission Confirmation
Category: SW 21.2(i) EvidenceRequired: If applicable
Email or receipt from UKVCAS confirming documents were submitted for the original Skilled Worker application. Further corroborates the application timeline.
11. Skilled Worker Visa Grant Letter
Category: SW 21.2(i) EvidenceRequired: If applicable
Official letter from the Home Office confirming the Skilled Worker visa was granted. Proves the fourth condition of SW 21.2(i) — that the application was approved.
12. Life in the UK Test Certificate
Category: Settlement RequirementRequired: Mandatory
Official certificate confirming the applicant passed the "Life in the UK" test. Mandatory settlement requirement under SW 23.1. Must show a pass result.
13. English Language Certificate (ECCTIS)
Category: Settlement RequirementRequired: Mandatory
ECCTIS (formerly NARIC) statement confirming the applicant's qualification was taught in English and/or meets the B1 CEFR level. Satisfies the English language requirement under SW 22A.
14. Academic Qualification Level Statement
Category: Settlement RequirementRequired: Supporting
ECCTIS statement confirming the level of the applicant's academic qualification in UK-equivalent terms. Supports the English language claim and general qualification evidence.
15. Employer Reference Letter
Category: Sponsorship / EmploymentRequired: Mandatory
Letter from the current employer (sponsor) confirming ongoing employment, job title, salary, that the role is still needed, and that the sponsor holds a valid licence. Must be issued within 28 days before ILR application submission. Satisfies SW 24.1–24.3.
16. Payslip (Recent Month)
Category: Financial EvidenceRequired: Mandatory
Most recent payslip showing gross salary, deductions, and net pay. Proves current earnings meet the salary threshold. Should be dated within 1 month of application.
17. P60 (Latest Tax Year)
Category: Financial EvidenceRequired: Mandatory
Annual P60 end-of-year certificate from the employer. Shows total earnings and tax paid for the latest full tax year. Corroborates salary level.
18. Bank Statement (Recent Month)
Category: Financial EvidenceRequired: Supporting
Bank statement showing salary credit matching the payslip. Proves the declared salary is genuinely received. Should cover the same period as the payslip.
19. Cover Letter
Category: Cover LetterRequired: Highly Recommended
A single comprehensive letter addressed to the caseworker. Should include: (1) Introduction to the application and which route/concession is being relied upon, (2) Immigration history summary — a chronological table of all visa grants, entries, and immigration events, (3) Absence declaration — listing every absence from the UK during the qualifying period with dates, destination, purpose, and number of days to prove absences are within the 180-day limit, (4) List of all documents enclosed, and (5) Proactive disclosure of any issues (e.g. government notices, passport stamp errors, prior visa refusals). Combining these into one letter makes it easier for the caseworker to follow.
20. Applicant Consent Form
Category: ConsentRequired: Mandatory
Signed consent form authorising the Home Office to process the application and verify information with third parties (employers, banks, HMRC, etc.).
21. Third-Party Consent Form
Category: ConsentRequired: If using a representative
Signed form authorising a third party (solicitor, representative, or family member) to act on behalf of the applicant and receive correspondence.
22. Council Tax Bill (Current Year)
Category: Residence EvidenceRequired: Mandatory
Current year's council tax bill showing the applicant's name and UK address. Proves physical residence in the UK. Also used as cohabitation proof for dependants at the same address.
23. Utility / Energy Bills (Recent)
Category: Residence EvidenceRequired: Supporting
Recent gas, electricity, or water bill at the UK address. Additional proof of UK residence. Strongest when in applicant's name or joint names.
24. Subsequent Visa Approval (Employer Change)
Category: Continuous PermissionRequired: If applicable
Grant letter for any subsequent Skilled Worker visa obtained after the initial grant (e.g. due to employer change). Proves continuous lawful permission with no gaps during the qualifying period.
25. Biometrics Appointment (Current ILR Application)
Category: Application AdminRequired: Supporting
Appointment confirmation for the ILR biometrics enrolment at a UKVCAS or TLS centre. Proof that the applicant attended (or will attend) the required biometrics appointment.
26. Government Notices (if any received)
Category: DisclosureRequired: If applicable
Any notices received from the Home Office, HMRC, DWP, local council, or other UK government agencies relating to immigration status, tax, benefits, or right to work. Even if issued in error, these should be disclosed transparently. Include the original notice.
27. Resolution / Cancellation Confirmation
Category: DisclosureRequired: If applicable
Official confirmation that the matter raised in any government notice was resolved, cancelled, or corrected. This is the "proof of closure" — always submit alongside the original notice to show the issue is fully resolved.
Dependant Wife Documents WIFE ▼
1. Current Passport (Bio + Stamped Pages)
Category: IdentityRequired: Mandatory
Full passport scan including bio page and all stamped pages. Proves the dependant wife's identity and travel history. Used to verify absences from the UK.
2. Old BRPs (All Previous)
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Scans of all previous BRPs. Shows continuous lawful leave as a dependant throughout the qualifying period, including any route changes (e.g. ICT Dependant to Skilled Worker Dependant).
3. eVisa Share Code
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Share code from gov.uk for independent verification of immigration status by the caseworker.
4. eVisa / Residence Permit Screenshot
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Screenshot from the UKVI online account showing current digital immigration status and conditions of leave.
5. Marriage Certificate
Category: RelationshipRequired: Mandatory
Official marriage certificate proving the legal relationship to the main applicant. If not in English, a certified translation must be provided alongside the original. This is the primary evidence of a genuine and subsisting relationship under SW 40.1.
6. Life in the UK Test Certificate
Category: Knowledge of LifeRequired: Mandatory
Certificate confirming the dependant wife passed the Life in the UK test. Mandatory settlement requirement. Must show a pass result with the correct name.
7. ECCTIS Certificate (English Language)
Category: English B1Required: Mandatory
ECCTIS statement confirming the qualification meets the English language requirement at B1 CEFR level or above. Primary evidence for the English language requirement.
8. Medium of Instruction Certificate (English)
Category: English B1 (supporting)Required: Supporting
Certificate from the university or educational institution confirming the degree was taught and examined in English. Supports the ECCTIS certificate.
9. Degree Certificate
Category: Qualification / EnglishRequired: Supporting
Copy of the degree certificate. Paired with the ECCTIS statement and Medium of Instruction certificate to provide triple evidence of English language proficiency.
10. Cover Letter
Category: Cover LetterRequired: Highly Recommended
Addressed to the caseworker. Introduces the dependant partner application, explains the relationship, summarises immigration history, states the qualifying period argument under SW 42.1 (including how ICT dependant time counts), lists documents enclosed, and discloses any relevant government notices or issues.
11. Declaration & Consent from Main Applicant
Category: DeclarationRequired: Recommended
Signed declaration from the settled main applicant (spouse) confirming the relationship is genuine and subsisting, confirming cohabitation, and consenting to verification checks on documents held in their name (e.g. joint utility bills).
12–14. Consent Forms (Parts 1, 2, 3)
Category: ConsentRequired: Mandatory
Standard UKVI consent forms signed by the applicant. Part 1 covers general consent, Part 2 covers data sharing, Part 3 covers third-party representation. All parts must be signed and submitted.
15–16. Government Notices & Resolution (if applicable) SHARED
Category: DisclosureRequired: If applicable
Any notices from the Home Office, HMRC, DWP, or other government agencies relating to immigration or residency, plus the resolution/cancellation confirmation. If the same notice affected the whole family, the same document can be submitted for each applicant. Must be disclosed and explained in the cover letter.
17. Council Tax Bill (Current Year) SHARED
Category: Residence / CohabitationRequired: Mandatory
Current year's council tax bill. Proves the applicant resides at the same address as the main applicant. Same document as submitted for the main applicant. Names on the bill strengthen the claim.
18–22. Council Tax Bills (Years 1–5 of Qualifying Period)
Category: Residence / CohabitationRequired: Recommended (1 per year)
Council tax bills covering each year of the 5-year qualifying period. Shows continuous residence at the same address throughout. Strongest evidence when the applicant's name appears on the bill. Even if in the main applicant's name only, it proves the household was established.
23–28. Energy / Utility Bills (spread across qualifying period)
Category: CohabitationRequired: Recommended (1–2 per year)
Gas, electricity, or water bills at the shared address. At least 1–2 per year across the qualifying period to show continuous cohabitation. Joint bills are ideal; bills in either spouse's name at the shared address are acceptable with a supporting declaration.
Dependant Child Documents CHILD ▼
1. Current Passport (Bio + Stamped Pages)
Category: IdentityRequired: Mandatory
Full passport scan of the child's current passport including bio page and any stamped pages. Proves identity and nationality.
2. Previous Passport (if applicable)
Category: IdentityRequired: If held
If the child has had a previous passport (e.g. expired or renewed during the qualifying period), include stamped pages to show travel history and original entry visa.
3–5. BRPs (All Previous + Current)
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Scans or photographs of all BRPs issued to the child. Shows continuous lawful leave as a dependant child. Include both old and current BRPs to demonstrate the full immigration history.
6. eVisa Share Code
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Share code from gov.uk for the child's immigration status. Generated from the parent's UKVI account on behalf of the child.
7. eVisa / Residence Permit Screenshot
Category: Immigration StatusRequired: Mandatory
Screenshot from the UKVI account showing the child's current digital immigration status and conditions of leave.
8. Birth Certificate
Category: Relationship to ParentsRequired: Mandatory
Official birth certificate proving the child's relationship to both parents (father and mother). If not in English, a certified English translation must accompany the original. This is the key document proving the child is the biological/legal child of the settled parents.
9. Cover Letter (on behalf of child)
Category: Cover LetterRequired: Highly Recommended
Written and signed by a parent on behalf of the child. Introduces the application, confirms both parents are settled (or applying), states the child lives with both parents, confirms financial support without public funds, discloses any relevant government notices or issues, and lists all enclosed documents.
10. Declaration from Both Parents
Category: DeclarationRequired: Recommended
Signed declaration from both parents confirming: the child lives with them, they will continue to support the child financially, the child does not rely on public funds, and they consent to the application being made on the child's behalf.
11–13. Consent Forms (Parts 1, 2, 3)
Category: ConsentRequired: Mandatory
Standard UKVI consent forms signed by a parent on behalf of the child (as the child is under 18). Covers general consent, data sharing, and third-party representation.
14–15. Government Notices & Resolution (if applicable) SHARED
Category: DisclosureRequired: If applicable
If the child (or the family as a whole) received any notices from the Home Office, HMRC, or other government agencies, include the original notice and resolution confirmation. Must be explained in the cover letter even if the notice was issued in error and subsequently cancelled.
16–17. School Admission / Attendance Letters
Category: UK ResidenceRequired: If applicable
Letters from the child's school confirming admission and/or attendance. Provides independent third-party proof that the child has been residing in the UK. Ideally include letters from different years to show continuous residence (e.g. initial admission + a recent year).
18. Cohabitation Proof — Energy Bill SHARED
Category: CohabitationRequired: Supporting
Recent energy/utility bill at the family address. Shows the child lives at the same address as both parents. Can be the same bill submitted for the wife's application.
19. Cohabitation Proof — Council Tax SHARED
Category: CohabitationRequired: Supporting
Current council tax bill at the family address. Shows the household where the child resides. Same document used across the family's applications.
20–22. Father's Passport + ILR eVisa + Share Code
Category: Parent Settled StatusRequired: Mandatory
Father's passport (identity), ILR eVisa screenshot, and share code proving the father has been granted Indefinite Leave to Remain. The share code allows the caseworker to verify settled status independently via the Home Office online checking service.
23–24. Mother's Passport + ILR eVisa / Share Code
Category: Parent Settled StatusRequired: Mandatory
Mother's passport (identity) and ILR eVisa screenshot or share code proving the mother has been granted ILR. Both parents being settled is a requirement for a dependant child's settlement application.
Documents NOT Required for Dependant Child (Under 18) ▼
Life in the UK Test
Children under 18 are exempt from the Life in the UK test requirement.
English Language Evidence
Children under 18 are exempt from the English language requirement.
Absence Declaration
The 180-day absence rule does not apply to dependant children under 18. No absence declaration is required.
5-Year Continuous Residence Proof
Children do not have their own 5-year qualifying period. They become eligible for ILR when both parents are settled or applying to settle.
Bank Statements / Payslips / P60
Financial evidence is not required for a dependant child. The parents' financial support is confirmed via declaration.
Council Tax / Utility Bills (in child's name)
Children will not have bills in their name. Cohabitation is proven through parents' bills and school letters.
General Tips for All Applicants ▼
  • Convert all .docx files to PDF before uploading
  • Convert BRP/passport photographs (JPG) to PDF for consistency
  • Keep individual files under 6 MB (compress large scans if needed)
  • Name files descriptively so the caseworker can identify them easily
  • Your cover letter is your chance to tell the story — make it clear, concise, and structured
  • Disclose everything proactively — government notices, stamp errors, prior refusals, anything unusual
  • When in doubt, include the document. More evidence is better than less.
